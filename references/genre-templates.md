# Genre-Specific GDD Guidance

Use this reference after the user selects a genre. Load only the relevant section mentally; do not force every question.

## RPG

Ask or infer:
- Combat model: real-time, turn-based, tactical, idle, hybrid.
- Progression: character level, gear, skills, companions, collection, story unlocks.
- Content structure: main story, side quests, dungeons, open world, stages.
- Build variety: classes, elements, skill trees, equipment affixes.
- Failure and recovery: death penalty, retry cost, checkpoint model.

Must define:
- Player growth loop.
- Role/build differentiation.
- Content production model.
- Progression economy.

## Card And Turn-Based

Ask or infer:
- Card acquisition: starter deck, draft, packs, unlocks, crafting.
- Deck construction constraints: deck size, copies, factions, costs, archetypes.
- Turn structure: energy/mana, actions, draw, discard, status effects.
- Rarity and balance model.
- PvE, PvP, roguelike run, or campaign structure.

Must define:
- Core decision tension.
- Card economy and progression.
- Balance knobs: cost, draw, tempo, rarity, synergy.
- First playable card pool size.

## Casual And Puzzle

Ask or infer:
- Session length and interruption model.
- Input simplicity and one-handed play needs.
- Level generation: handcrafted, procedural, hybrid.
- Difficulty ramp and fail state.
- Monetization tolerance: ads, remove ads, boosters, cosmetics.

Must define:
- First 30 seconds experience.
- Level goal structure.
- Difficulty ramp.
- Replay and retention hooks.

## Shooter And Action

Ask or infer:
- Camera and control scheme.
- Combat time-to-kill, enemy density, movement speed.
- Weapon/combo identity.
- Level/map rhythm.
- Skill expression: aim, timing, positioning, build choice.

Must define:
- Combat feel target.
- Control layout and input priority.
- Encounter design loop.
- Weapon/enemy matrix.

## Music And Rhythm

Ask or infer:
- Input model: tap, hold, slide, gesture, instrument-like, motion.
- Chart style: falling notes, lanes, radial, freeform, rhythm combat.
- Music source: licensed tracks, original tracks, procedural, user-generated.
- Difficulty model: speed, density, timing windows, modifiers.
- Session structure: single song, campaign, daily challenge, PvP score race.

Must define:
- Timing window and scoring model.
- Track/content production plan.
- Difficulty ladder.
- Audio latency calibration needs.

## Sports And Racing

Ask or infer:
- Sport/race type and realism level.
- Control model: arcade, simulation, manager, hybrid.
- Session length and match/race format.
- Progression: athletes, vehicles, teams, leagues, upgrades.
- Competition model: AI, local PvP, online PvP, asynchronous leaderboard.

Must define:
- Moment-to-moment skill expression.
- Match/race pacing.
- Progression and upgrade economy.
- Fairness and rubber-band/comeback rules.

## Horror And Puzzle

Ask or infer:
- Horror emphasis: survival, psychological, chase, atmosphere, body horror, suspense.
- Puzzle model: inventory, logic, spatial, physics, narrative clue, escape room.
- Failure model: death, restart, resource loss, narrative consequence.
- Tension curve and safe-room pacing.
- Content format: rooms, chapters, cases, open location.

Must define:
- Fear/tension target.
- Puzzle clue chain and hint system.
- Resource pressure or fail-state rules.
- Pacing between tension, relief, and discovery.

## Simulation And Management

Ask or infer:
- Resource chain: sources, sinks, conversion, storage.
- Time model: real-time, turn-based, idle, calendar.
- Expansion model: buildings, workers, territory, recipes, tech tree.
- Failure pressure: debt, demand, decay, competitors, disasters.
- Automation and late-game complexity.

Must define:
- Resource economy diagram.
- Progression unlock chain.
- Player planning horizon.
- Production bottlenecks and balancing knobs.

## Strategy

Ask or infer:
- Decision layer: macro economy, tactics, deck/army build, diplomacy, positioning.
- Information model: perfect, fog of war, hidden hands, random events.
- Match/session length.
- AI/PvP expectations.
- Win/loss conditions.

Must define:
- Strategic choice model.
- Counterplay and comeback mechanisms.
- Unit/card/build taxonomy.
- Match pacing and snowball control.
