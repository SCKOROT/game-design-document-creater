# GDD Quality Rubric

Use this rubric when diagnosing an existing GDD or reviewing a newly generated one. Score out of 100 and explain the top improvement opportunities.

| Dimension | Points | What Good Looks Like |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| Core experience | 15 | The player feeling/fantasy is specific and every major design choice supports it. |
| Core loop | 15 | The repeated player actions are clear, testable, and compelling within 3-6 steps. |
| System coherence | 15 | Major systems reinforce each other instead of forming an unrelated feature list. |
| Team-size fit | 10 | Scope, art style, tech, and schedule match the stated team size. |
| Market differentiation | 10 | Competitors are named and the design has concrete differences. |
| Numerical framework | 10 | Core resources, progression, economy, and balancing assumptions are present. |
| Core metrics | 10 | Metrics match the project type, business model, and validation stage. |
| Milestone feasibility | 10 | MVP, vertical slice, and release phases have clear deliverables and validation criteria. |
| Risk quality | 5 | Risks include cause, impact, mitigation, and early warning signals. |

## Scoring Anchors

Use anchors to keep scoring consistent:

| Dimension | Full Score | Passing Score | Low Score |
|-----------|------------|---------------|-----------|
| Core experience | Specific emotional/fantasy target and mechanics clearly support it. | Experience target exists but is broad or only partly reflected in systems. | No clear player feeling, fantasy, or experience target. |
| Core loop | 3-6 step loop is clear, testable, and differentiated. | Loop exists but has vague rewards, weak repetition, or unclear pacing. | Only genre labels or feature list, no repeatable loop. |
| System coherence | Systems reinforce one another with clear priorities. | Some systems connect, but priorities or dependencies are unclear. | Systems are unrelated, duplicated, or purely additive. |
| Team-size fit | Scope, art, tech, and schedule are realistic for the team. | Some scope risk exists but can be cut. | Plan is clearly too large or too complex for the team. |
| Market differentiation | Competitors and concrete differences are named. | Competitors named, but differentiation is generic. | No competitor context or only "more fun/better art" claims. |
| Numerical framework | Resources, progression, economy, and balancing knobs are defined. | Basic resources/progression exist but lack tuning levers. | No usable numerical or economy framework. |
| Core metrics | Metrics fit project stage, team, and business model. | Metrics exist but are too generic or incomplete. | Metrics are absent or mismatched. |
| Milestone feasibility | MVP, vertical slice, deliverables, and validation criteria are concrete. | Phases exist but deliverables or validation are vague. | Timeline is a wish list without testable milestones. |
| Risk quality | Cause, impact, mitigation, and warning signals are specific. | Risks are named with basic mitigation. | Risks are generic or missing. |

## Score Bands

- 90-100: Production-ready planning baseline.
- 75-89: Strong concept, needs targeted tightening.
- 60-74: Usable draft, but important planning gaps remain.
- 40-59: Concept direction exists, but execution risk is high.
- 0-39: Needs restructuring before production planning.
