# GDD Quality Rubric

Use this rubric when diagnosing an existing GDD or reviewing a newly generated one. Score out of 100 and explain the top improvement opportunities.

| Dimension | Points | What Good Looks Like |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| Core experience | 15 | The player feeling/fantasy is specific and every major design choice supports it. |
| Core loop | 15 | The repeated player actions are clear, testable, and compelling within 3-6 steps. |
| System coherence | 15 | Major systems reinforce each other instead of forming an unrelated feature list. |
| Team-size fit | 10 | Scope, art style, tech, and schedule match the stated team size. |
| Market differentiation | 10 | Competitors are named and the design has concrete differences. |
| Numerical framework | 10 | Core resources, progression, economy, and balancing assumptions are present. |
| Core metrics | 10 | Metrics match the project type, business model, and validation stage. |
| Milestone feasibility | 10 | MVP, vertical slice, and release phases have clear deliverables and validation criteria. |
| Risk quality | 5 | Risks include cause, impact, mitigation, and early warning signals. |

## Score Bands

- 90-100: Production-ready planning baseline.
- 75-89: Strong concept, needs targeted tightening.
- 60-74: Usable draft, but important planning gaps remain.
- 40-59: Concept direction exists, but execution risk is high.
- 0-39: Needs restructuring before production planning.
